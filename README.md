Simple Interest Calculator
Description

A Simple Interest Calculator is a simple project used to calculate the interest on a principal amount based on the rate of interest and time period.

Formula

Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

P = Principal Amount
R = Rate of Interest
T = Time Period (in years)
Features
Easy to use.
Calculates simple interest quickly.
Accepts principal amount.
Accepts rate of interest.
Accepts time period.
Displays simple interest and total amount.
Example

Input:

Principal Amount = 1000
Rate of Interest = 5%
Time = 2 years

Calculation:

SI = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Output:

Simple Interest = 100
Total Amount = 1100
Technologies Used
Basic Mathematics
Programming Fundamentals
How to Use
Enter the principal amount.
Enter the rate of interest.
Enter the time period in years.
Apply the simple interest formula.
Display the calculated interest.
